What’s in this pool — and what isn’t

This simulator uses the $108.9M in DLGF-reviewed city funds — the general fund plus city-controlled Local Income Tax (LIT) funds. Excluded: Utilities ($57.6M, ratepayer-funded), Transit ($19M, federal grants), and dedicated road/solid waste funds ($10.6M Motor Vehicle Highway + Local Road & Street + Solid Waste). Controller McClellan presented $110.2M as the comparable DLGF total to the council in October 2025.
